Last Saturday we planted **beans, peas and sunflowers** along the south fence.

## What went well

- Everyone found a job, from sowing to watering.
- The children made labels for every row.
- We shared a long lunch in the sun.

## What is next

The seedlings need water twice a week. A rota is pinned inside the tool shed.
