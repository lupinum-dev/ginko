## What we do

We share a plot of land behind the old mill. Members grow vegetables and flowers, swap seeds, and look after a small orchard together.

## Who can join

Everyone who lives nearby is welcome. You do not need any experience. Bring gloves and curiosity; we lend the tools.

## How it is run

Decisions are made at an open meeting on the first Monday of each month. Costs are covered by small yearly contributions and the occasional plant sale.
