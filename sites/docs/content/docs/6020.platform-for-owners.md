---
title: The platform for owners
description: What owners do in the Studio. Sign-in, roles, save and publish, approval, schedule and history.
icon: lucide:users
---

The platform is a web app that you [host yourself]($docs.host-the-platform). It turns your bricks into forms and stores the content. The site fetches the content from it as a bundle. Without the platform, a site reads all content from files.

- **Sign-in.** People sign in with a code that arrives by email. An account exists only by invitation.
- **Roles.** An **owner** (`owner`) may do everything on their site and invites staff. A **staff member** (`staff`) may only do the tasks that the owner grants. An **admin** may do everything on all sites.
- **Editing.** Owners change entries in forms. Tasks from the bricks are shortcuts for frequent chores. A preview shows the real site with the changed content.
- **Save and publish.** Saving puts nothing online: a new entry is a draft, and changes to an entry or single that is online wait as unpublished changes. "Publish …" puts them online now or at a time, "Discard" goes back to the version on the website. An entry of a collection can go back to a draft (offline, without deleting it); a single is always online. A task on Today publishes with one press on "Publish". Moves in the navigation tree go online at once.
- **Safety.** If two people change the same field, the platform detects the conflict. A change can be undone. Deleted entries can be restored for 30 days.
- **Approval.** If you want, a task or an agent key needs a yes before a change goes live. The reviewer sees the change and approves it, approves it for a time, or rejects it with a sentence; the author sees the reason. Approval is off until someone turns it on.
- **Schedule.** A draft or unpublished changes go live at a set time. A release publishes several changes together.
- **History.** The history shows every change, with "Show changes" and "Back to this version".
- **Translations.** If someone changes a text in the main locale, the translation counts as outdated. The site keeps showing it until someone confirms or rewrites it; the Studio lists it.
- **Refresh now.** The site reloads content at most every 60 seconds. An owner can have a site reload at once, for example to take an entry off the site. The site needs a token for this (see [Environment variables]($docs.environment-variables)).

You, the developer, define the bricks. The owners fill them.
