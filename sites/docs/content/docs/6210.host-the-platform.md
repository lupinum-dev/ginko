The platform is the part of Ginko where owners and staff change content: the Studio, a web app, and its backend on [Convex](https://www.convex.dev). Websites load their content from it. You run the platform yourself, and this section shows how.

::callout{type="note"}
Lupinum runs a platform for its own clients. It is not a public service.
::

## What hosting it means

- **Your accounts.** The backend runs on your Convex account, the Studio on your Vercel account, and sign-in mail goes through your Resend account. The content stays there.
- **You decide who signs in.** The platform has no open sign-up. The admin email you choose signs in first, creates the sites and invites the owners.
- **You run the updates.** A new version reaches your platform when you raise the versions in the project and deploy.

You create a platform project, try it on your computer, and then put it online. The pages of this section have the steps.

## What you need to host it

- **Node** 22.18 or later, 24 or 26, and **npm**.
- **A [Convex](https://www.convex.dev) account.** The backend runs there.
- **A [Vercel](https://vercel.com) account.** The Studio runs there.
- **A [Resend](https://resend.com) account** and a domain you can verify there. People sign in with a code that arrives by email, and Resend sends it.

You need the accounts only to put the platform online. On your computer, the platform runs without any account.

## What is supported

| | Tested | Not tested |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backend | Convex cloud | Convex's self-hosted backend |
| Studio | Vercel | other hosts |
| Sign-in mail | Resend | other mail services |
| Package manager | npm; pnpm with the `pnpm-workspace.yaml` the project comes with | yarn, bun |

"Not tested" means: it may work, but nobody has checked it, and the commands on these pages assume the tested setup.

## The license

Ginko is open source under the MIT license, the platform included. You may host it for yourself and for your clients.

## The steps

1. [Start the platform on your computer]($docs.platform-on-your-computer): create the project and sign in to a local Studio.
2. [Put the platform online]($docs.put-the-platform-online): one command creates the Convex and Vercel projects and deploys.
3. [Connect a website]($docs.connect-a-website): create a site in the Studio and send it the website's schema.
4. [Update and back up the platform]($docs.update-the-platform): raise the versions and deploy.
