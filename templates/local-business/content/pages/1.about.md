## How we work

Each session is one to one, with the same therapist from the first appointment to the last. We take time to find the cause of your complaint, explain what we find in plain words, and give you exercises that fit into your day. You leave each session knowing what to do until the next one.

## The practice

Three treatment rooms and a training room with weights, balance boards and a treadmill, on the ground floor and step-free. [How to find us and where to park]($page.contact).

## Who treats you

Three physiotherapists with further training in manual therapy, sports physiotherapy and lymphatic drainage. [Meet the team]($page.team).

## Work with us

We are looking for a physiotherapist for 20 to 30 hours a week. Ask us at the front desk, or [write to us]($page.contact).
