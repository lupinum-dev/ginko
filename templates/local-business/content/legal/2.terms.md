## Appointments

You book an appointment by phone or by email, and we confirm the date and time the same way. The time is reserved
for you alone.

## Payment

The price of each session is on the [treatments page]($page.services). You pay after each session, by card or in cash
at the front desk. You get an invoice, which you can send to your health insurance with your doctor’s prescription.

## Cancellation

Please cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment, by phone or email; until then cancelling is free. For an
appointment cancelled later or missed, we charge the full price, because no one else can take the time at such short
notice.
