A classic massage warms and loosens tight muscles and eases the pain they cause. We often combine it with physiotherapy, so the muscles stay loose once you move again.

## Good to know

Tell us before the session if you take blood thinners, are pregnant or have a skin condition: then we adapt the massage or suggest another treatment.
