A torn ligament, a strained muscle or a new knee need more than rest. We plan your way back in stages, test strength and balance at each one, and tell you when you can run, jump and play again.

## For whom

Runners, cyclists, skiers and team players at any level, and anyone who wants to be active again after an operation.

## In the training room

Part of each session takes place in our training room, with weights, a balance board and a treadmill, so you train the movements your sport asks for.
