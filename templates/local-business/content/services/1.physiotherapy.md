Your first session starts with questions: where it hurts, since when, and what makes it better or worse. Then your therapist examines how you move and explains what they find.

## What we do

- mobilise stiff joints and loosen tight muscles
- train the muscles that protect your back and joints
- show you a few exercises to do at home, so the treatment keeps working between sessions

## How many sessions

Most complaints need a series of six to ten sessions, once or twice a week. After the first session we tell you what to expect.
