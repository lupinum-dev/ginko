Lymphatic drainage uses slow, light strokes that push fluid towards working lymph nodes. It eases swelling and the feeling of tightness, and it does not hurt.

## When it helps

- swelling after an operation or an injury
- lymphoedema, for example after breast cancer treatment
- heavy, swollen legs from a vein disorder

With lymphoedema, the treatment works best several times a week at first, with a compression bandage or garment between sessions.
