Manual therapy finds out which joint or nerve causes your pain and treats it with precise movements of the therapist’s hands. It helps with a stiff neck, a blocked back, a frozen shoulder and jaw pain.

## What to expect

Your therapist tests each segment of the spine or the joint, then moves it gently in the direction it lacks. Many patients move more freely after the first session; exercises at home keep the gain.
