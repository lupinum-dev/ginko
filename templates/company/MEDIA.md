The share cards (1200 × 630) are generated by the site (nuxt-og-image). Inter is under the
[SIL Open Font License](https://openfontlicense.org).

When you add a photo, put it in `public/media/`, give it alt text in every language file that shows it, and add a
row here. The sample photos show other places and machines. Replace them with photos of your own works, products and
team (with their permission): buyers trust a maker they can see.
