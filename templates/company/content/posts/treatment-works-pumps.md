The inlet pumping station lifts all the town's sewage to the first stage of the treatment works. Its six pumps were more than thirty years old.

## One pump at a time

The works could not stop, so we changed one pump a week. Our team lifted the old pump out on its guide rail, set the new one in and started it up the same day. The other five carried the flow.

## What changed

The new pumps have vortex impellers that pass the wipes and fibres which blocked the old ones several times a month. They also run with frequency converters, so they follow the inflow instead of switching on and off.

We thank the staff of the works for six good weeks of work together.
