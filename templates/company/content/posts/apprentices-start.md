On 1 September five young people started their training with us: four as industrial mechanics and one as an electronics technician for industrial engineering.

In their first weeks they learn the basics in our training workshop. From the second year on they work in assembly, on the test bench and in the electrical workshop, and in the third year they go out with our service technicians.

Applications for next year's places are open now: see [jobs]($page.jobs).
