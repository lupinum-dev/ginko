Sewage can give off gases that make the air in a shaft explosive. Pumps there must be built so that they cannot ignite it.

From now on every size of our TS series is also available in a version approved for ATEX zone 1. The motor housing is flameproof, and sensors switch the pump off before the motor gets too hot or water reaches the windings.

The new version keeps the dimensions of the standard pump, so it fits existing guide rails. The [product sheet]($page.downloads) lists the sizes.
