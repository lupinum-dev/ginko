A pump does not choose its day to stop. From July on, our service line answers on Saturdays from 8:00 to 12:00 as well as on weekdays from 7:00 to 17:00.

On Saturdays we take your call, help you on the phone where we can, and plan a technician for the next working day. For customers with a service contract and an agreed standby, the standby number stays the same.

You find the service line and its email on our [contact page]($page.team).
