General terms of sale for business customers usually cover:

- how a contract comes about: quote, order and order confirmation
- prices (net, plus VAT), payment terms and retention of title
- delivery times, shipping, and who bears the risk in transit
- warranty for defects, liability and its limits
- returns, repairs and spare parts
- the place of jurisdiction and the applicable law
