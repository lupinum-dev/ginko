A pump that runs too long at the wrong point wears out early and uses more power than it needs. In two hours our technician checks one pump of any make and tells you what to do.

## What we check

- vibration and bearing noise
- the shaft seal and the coupling
- the motor current against the rating plate
- the duty point against the pump curve

You get a short report with the readings and what we recommend: nothing, a repair, or a pump that fits your system better.
