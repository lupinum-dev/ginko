The HK series is our most common pump: a single-stage centrifugal pump with an end suction and a top discharge. Its dimensions follow EN 733, so it replaces a pump of another maker without new pipework.

## Where it works

- water supply and booster stations
- cooling and heating circuits
- irrigation and fountains
- process water in food and drink plants

## How we size it

Send us the flow rate, the head and the fluid. We choose the size, the impeller diameter and the motor, and send you the pump curve with the quote. Most pumps leave our works within three weeks.
