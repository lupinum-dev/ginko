Send us the pump, or our service collects it. We take it apart, measure the wear and send you a quote with photos. You decide whether we repair it; if not, we send it back or scrap it for you.

## After the repair

Each pump runs on our test bench and leaves with a test report. For pumps that must not stop, we lend you a replacement pump while yours is in the workshop.
