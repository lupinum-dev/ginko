TS pumps work under water, in the shaft of a pumping station or in a sump. A vortex impeller passes solids and fibres without clogging; a channel impeller pumps more efficiently where the water carries less.

## Options

- guide rail system: lift the pump out without entering the shaft
- moisture and temperature sensors in the motor
- cable lengths up to 30 m

Every TS pump runs on our test bench before it leaves the works.
