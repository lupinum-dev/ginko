A pumping station lifts sewage or rainwater where it cannot flow by gravity. We deliver it as one unit: the shaft with the pumps, valves and pipework inside, and the control cabinet that switches the pumps by level and sends an alarm to your phone or control room.

## What you get

- a station sized for your inflow, with the calculation
- installation on site with your contractor, or by our own team
- commissioning and training of your staff
- a service contract, if you want one

## Where they run

Our stations serve villages, new housing areas, industrial estates and road underpasses. Ask us for references near you.
