Our customers are towns, water boards and industrial plants. When one of their pumps stops, you are the person who gets it running again.

## What you do

- start up new pumps and pumping stations and show the customer's staff how to run them
- find faults on site and repair them, or bring the pump to our workshop
- carry out service contracts: checks, measurements and small repairs
- write a short report on the tablet after each visit

## What you bring

- training as an industrial mechanic, mechatronics technician or electrician, or similar
- a driving licence (category B)
- calm when a customer is under pressure

## What we offer

A permanent contract, a company van you may also use privately, standby duty only every fifth week, and paid training at the makers of our motors and controls.
