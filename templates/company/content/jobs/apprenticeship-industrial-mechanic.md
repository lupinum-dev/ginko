You learn how a pump is built, from the raw casting to the test run, and how to repair it when it comes back.

## What you learn

- turning, milling and drilling, by hand and on CNC machines
- assembling pumps and motors
- running and measuring pumps on our test bench
- going out with our service technicians in your third year

## What you bring

- a school-leaving certificate (Realschulabschluss or Hauptschulabschluss)
- interest in machines and how they work

Send us a short letter, your CV and your last two school reports. We invite you to a trial day in the workshop.
