You join Katrin Albers and her team, who look after our municipal customers in the north and east of Germany.

## What you do

- size pumps and pumping stations from inflow, head and site plans
- write quotes and answer the questions of engineering offices
- visit customers and building sites, about two days a week
- hand finished orders over to our works and follow them up to commissioning

## What you bring

- a degree or a technician's qualification in mechanical, civil or water engineering
- some years in sales or planning, ideally with pumps or wastewater
- good German, written and spoken

## What we offer

A permanent contract, three days a week at home if you like, a company car and a team that answers questions.
