## What we make

We build centrifugal pumps, submersible pumps and complete pumping stations, and we look after them for as long as they run. Most of our pumps go to towns and water boards; the rest to industrial plants that move water, cooling liquids or process water.

## How we work

Every pump is assembled in our works in Bielefeld and runs on our own test bench before it leaves. Our engineers size each pump for its system, because a pump that fits uses less power and lasts longer. Our service team repairs pumps of any make, also ones we did not build.

## Who we are

A family company with a workshop, an assembly hall, a test bench and a training workshop for our apprentices. You can [see our open jobs]($page.jobs) or [talk to sales]($page.team).
