A privacy policy for a conference website usually covers:

- who is responsible for the website, with address and contact details
- which personal data the website and the conference process and why, for example server logs at your host, emails from guests, the names on the badges, or photos and recordings at the conference
- the legal basis, and how long you keep the data
- which services you use (hosting, email, the ticket shop) and where they process data; the ticket shop has its own privacy policy, link to it
- the rights of visitors: access, correction, deletion, objection, and a complaint to the Austrian data protection authority (Datenschutzbehörde)
