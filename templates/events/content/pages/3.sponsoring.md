## What your support pays for

The ticket price does not cover what the conference costs. Sponsors pay for the rest: reduced tickets for students, people without income and volunteers, live captions in the Festsaal, the quiet room, and the recordings.

## What sponsors get

Your logo on the home page and in the Festsaal, a table in the foyer on both days, and tickets for your team. Sponsors do not choose talks or speakers: the programme team decides on its own.
