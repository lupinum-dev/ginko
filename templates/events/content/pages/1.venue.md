## Address

Kulturhaus am Kanal\
Beispielgasse 12\
1020 Wien

[Open in maps](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query=Beispielgasse%2012%2C%201020%20Wien)

## Getting there

The venue is a short walk from the underground and the tram. Come by public transport or by bicycle: there are racks at the entrance, and there is no car park for guests.

## Getting in

The main entrance on Beispielgasse has no steps. Badges are handed out in the foyer from 09:00 on the first day; bring your ticket on your phone or on paper.

## Rooms

- **Festsaal**, ground floor: keynote, talks and the panel. It has an induction loop.
- **Galerie**, first floor: talks for smaller groups.
- **Studio**, first floor: the workshops. Bring a laptop.
- **Foyer**, ground floor: coffee, lunch and the sponsors' tables.

A lift reaches every floor, and each floor has accessible toilets. A quiet room on the first floor is open on both days.
