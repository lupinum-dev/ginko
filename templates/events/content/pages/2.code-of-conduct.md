## What we expect

Be kind and listen. Respect people whatever their gender, origin, age, body, abilities, religion or experience. Ask before you take a photo of someone, and respect a lanyard that says “no photos”.

## What we do not accept

Harassment in any form: insults, unwanted contact or attention, intimidation, following someone, sexual images or jokes, and disrupting talks. This applies in every room, at the evening drinks and online.

## What happens when someone breaks it

During the conference, call our awareness team on [+43 1 012 78 91](tel:+4310127891), from 08:30 to 22:00 on both days, including the evening drinks. Eva Lindner and Paul Kerschbaum answer; you can also find them at the registration desk in the foyer, or speak to anyone in a team shirt.

Before or after the conference, write to us (the address is at the foot of every page); we answer within one working day.

We listen, we keep what you tell us to ourselves, and we act: from a warning to asking a person to leave without a refund.

## Who it applies to

Everyone at the conference: guests, speakers, sponsors, volunteers and the team.
