Two days about data that everyone may use: what cities, libraries and researchers publish, how journalists and developers work with it, and what still gets in the way.

The first day starts with talks on budgets, metadata and privacy and ends with short talks from the community. The second day turns to statistics and transport data and closes with a panel on who pays for open data. Workshops in the Studio run on both days and have fewer places.

The ticket covers both days, lunch and coffee. Students, people without income and volunteers pay less: the ticket shop has the prices.
