The share cards (1200 × 630) are generated by the site (nuxt-og-image) with Inter
([SIL Open Font License](https://openfontlicense.org)).

When you add a photo, put it in `public/media/`, give it alt text in every language file that shows it, and add a
row here. The sample photos are stock photos of other events and another building. Replace them with photos of your
own conference and venue (people only with their permission). A speaker needs no photo: without one the site shows
their initials.
