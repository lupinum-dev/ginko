## What we do

We clean the river bank several times a year, test the water with schools, and plant native shrubs where the bank is bare. Everyone can join in, members or not.

## How we are organised

River Commons is a registered association (eingetragener Verein). The members’ meeting elects the board. We have one part-time coordinator; everything else is done by volunteers. We pay for our work with membership fees, donations and small grants.

## What we stand for

- The river belongs to everyone, so everyone is welcome.
- We work with the town, the landowners and the anglers, not against them.
- We report what we do and what it costs, in our annual report.
