## Donation receipts

River Commons is recognised as a non-profit organisation (gemeinnützig). For donations of more than 300 euros, we send you a donation receipt for your tax return. For smaller amounts, your bank statement is enough for the tax office in Germany.

## What your donation pays for

Gloves and grabbers wear out, test kits need replacing, and our coordinator plans every cleanup. Our [annual report]($page.downloads) shows what we spent, and on what.
