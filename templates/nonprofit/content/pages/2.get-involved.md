## Come to a cleanup

Our cleanups are open to everyone, and you do not need to sign up. We bring gloves, grabbers and bags; you bring sturdy shoes and clothes that may get dirty. Every date is on our [events page]($page.events), and members also get it by email.

## Become a member

Members vote at the members’ meeting and get our news by email. Write to us (our email address is at the top of this page) and we will send you the membership form.

## Help with what you know

We are glad of help with water tests, photos, repairs to our tools, and translations into the languages spoken in our town. Tell [Lukas, our volunteer coordinator,](mailto:lukas@example.com) what you would like to do.
