## Come to a clean-up

Our clean-ups are open to everyone, and you do not need to sign up. We bring gloves, grabbers and bags; you bring sturdy shoes and clothes that may get dirty. Here are the [next dates](/events).

## Become a member

Members vote at the members’ meeting and get our news by email. Write to [hello@example.com](mailto:hello@example.com) and we will send you the membership form.

## Help with what you know

We are glad of help with water tests, photos, repairs to our tools, and translations into the languages spoken in our town. Tell [Lukas, our volunteer coordinator,](mailto:lukas@example.com) what you would like to do.
