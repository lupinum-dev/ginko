On a Saturday morning in September, volunteers of all ages cleared the river bank between the two bridges.

## What we found

Most of it was small: bottle caps, cigarette filters and pieces of plastic wrap. These are the things that reach the sea if nobody picks them up. There were larger finds too, among them a shopping trolley and two tyres.

## What happens next

The town's waste service collected the full bags on Monday. We have told the town where rubbish collects after high water, and we will check those places again at the winter clean-up.

Thank you to everyone who came.
