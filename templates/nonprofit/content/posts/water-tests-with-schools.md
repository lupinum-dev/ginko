Pupils from a secondary school near the river now test the water with us once a month. They measure temperature, pH and nitrate with simple test kits and add the results to a shared table.

## Why we do it

One measurement says little. Many measurements over a year show how the river changes after heavy rain, in a hot summer or when the fields are fertilised.

## How to join

Teachers who would like to do this with their class can write to [Lukas](mailto:lukas@example.com). We bring the test kits and show how they work.
