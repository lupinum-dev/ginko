> Replace this with your own details. In Germany, a website like this one needs an imprint (Impressum) under the Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz (DDG) and, for news, the Medienstaatsvertrag (MStV). This page is a placeholder from the template, not legal advice.

An imprint usually names:

- the name of the association and its legal form, with the full address
- the members of the board who represent the association
- the register court and the register number (Vereinsregister)
- an email address and a phone number
- the VAT number, if the association has one
- the person responsible for the content of the news
