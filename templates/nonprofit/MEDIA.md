The share cards (1200 × 630) are generated by the site (nuxt-og-image), with Noto Sans Arabic for Arabic pages
([SIL Open Font License](https://openfontlicense.org)); Inter is under the same license.

When you add a photo, put it in `public/media/`, give it alt text in every language file that shows it, and add a
row here. The sample photos are stock photos of other places. Replace them with photos of your own work: your
river, your volunteers (with their permission), your clean-ups. Visitors trust a group they can see. A post needs
no photo: the news list and the post page work without one.
