Before a joinery cuts any wood for us, we draw the piece at full size on paper: the chair from the side, from the front and from above, with every joint.

On a screen, a curve can look right at any size. On paper at 1:1 you can hold a real cushion against it, sit on a stool beside it and see whether the back meets you where it should. For the Holm chair, the full-size drawing showed that the back was two centimetres too high before we built the first prototype.

The drawings go with the order to the workshop. The joiners mark them up, we answer on the same sheet, and in the end the paper holds every decision of the project.
