The architects had built a fine concrete stair, but the hall was loud and visitors did not know where to go. We kept the concrete as it is and added only what the hall lacked.

The reception desk is a single block of dark stone, set where people look first when they come in. Panels of wool felt on the back wall take the echo out of the room. The signs are cut into the concrete at the foot of the stair, so they need no frames or screens.

We worked with the architects from the start of the fit-out; the hall opened with the building.
