Holm began as a chair for the attic flat in Ottensen: the client wanted chairs light enough to carry up the stairs and strong enough for a long life at the table.

The seat and back are bent from oak veneer; the legs and rails are solid oak, joined with dowels and glue, without screws or brackets. We built five prototypes before the curve of the back was right for long dinners.

The joinery makes the chair to order in oak, oiled or soaped.
