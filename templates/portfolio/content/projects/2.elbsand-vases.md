The series started with walks along the Elbe in winter: pale sand, grey reeds and the rust of old groynes. We wanted vases that hold a single branch as well as a full bunch, and that look calm together on one shelf.

Ole drew the nine shapes, and the pottery threw them on the wheel. Each glaze took several rounds of tests; we kept the ones that change with the thickness of the glaze, so no two vases are quite the same.

The vases are made in small batches. Shops and restaurants can order them in sets.
