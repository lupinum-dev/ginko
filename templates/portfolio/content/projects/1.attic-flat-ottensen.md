The flat had small roof windows and low light all day. We moved the bedroom to the back and gave the living room a band of high windows facing south-west, so the afternoon sun reaches deep into the room.

The walls are lime plaster in a warm grey that takes the light softly. Along the low side of the roof we built cupboards, a desk and a bench in oiled oak, so the slope holds the storage and the room stays open.

We drew every built-in piece at full size and made it with joiners in Hamburg. The client lived in the flat during the work; the building work took eleven weeks.
