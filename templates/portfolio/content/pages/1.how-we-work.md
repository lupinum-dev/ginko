## A first conversation

Write or call us and tell us about your space and what you want to change. We meet you there or in the studio, look at the rooms together and ask about how you live or work in them. The first conversation costs nothing.

## Concept and fee

After the visit we send you a short concept with sketches and a fixed fee for the design. You decide then whether we go on.

## Drawings and samples

We draw the rooms and every piece of furniture, and we bring samples of the materials so you can see and touch them in your own light. Once you agree, we ask the workshops for their prices.

## Making and fitting

We order the work from joiners, plasterers and potters we know, and we visit them while they make it. We are there for the fitting and come back once more after a few weeks to see how it works in daily life.

The [projects]($page.projects) show what this looks like in practice.
