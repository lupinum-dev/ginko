> Replace this with your own details. In Austria, a business website needs an imprint (information under the E-Commerce Act and the Media Act). This page is a placeholder from the template, not legal advice.

An imprint usually names:

- the name of the business and its legal form, address, phone and email
- the company register number and the register court, if the business is registered
- the VAT number
- the trade authority and the membership of the Chamber of Commerce, where they apply
- the owner of the website and what it is about (disclosure under the Media Act)
