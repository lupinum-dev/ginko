A privacy policy usually covers:

- who is responsible for the website, with address and contact details
- which personal data the website processes and why, for example server logs at your host, or emails and calls for reservations
- the legal basis, and how long you keep the data
- which services you use to run the site (hosting, email) and where they process data
- the rights of visitors: access, correction, deletion, objection, and a complaint to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde)
