## By phone

We answer during our opening hours (below). Outside them, please write to us.

## By email

Write to us with the day, the time and how many of you are coming. We confirm your table by email.

## Groups and celebrations

For groups of 10 or more, please call us, so the kitchen can plan for you. We are happy to put together a set menu.

## If your plans change

Please let us know, so we can give the table to other guests.
