## Our house

We are a family-run Gasthaus. We cook the dishes Vienna grew up on: schnitzel, Tafelspitz, goulash, strudel. We make them the way we like to eat them ourselves, with time and good butter.

## Our kitchen

We buy meat, vegetables and wine from producers we know, as close to Vienna as we can. The menu changes a little with the seasons, and from Tuesday to Friday there is a dish of the day at lunchtime.

## Our garden

From spring to autumn we serve outside, in the garden under the chestnut trees. When it rains, there is room for everyone inside.

## Allergies

Our menu shows the allergens of every dish with the EU letters. If you have an allergy or an intolerance, please tell us when you order. We are glad to help.
