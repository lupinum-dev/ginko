## By phone

Call us on [+43 1 000 00 00](tel:+4310000000). We answer during our opening hours.

## By email

Write to [tisch@example.com](mailto:tisch@example.com) with the day, the time and how many of you are coming. We confirm your table by email.

## Groups and celebrations

For groups of 10 or more, please call us. We are happy to put together a set menu for you.

## If your plans change

Please let us know, so we can give the table to other guests.
