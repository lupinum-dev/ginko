## Who I am

I grew up in Freiburg and Edinburgh and have translated between German and English since 2014. Before that I
worked for six years as an editor at an art book publisher.

## What I work on

Most of my work comes from museums and research institutes: wall texts, catalogues, papers and reports. For every
text that goes to print, a native-speaker proofreader reads it after me.

## Working together

Write to me with the text, the deadline and where it will appear. I answer every request within two working days.
