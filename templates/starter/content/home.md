## What I translate

- Exhibition and catalogue texts for museums and galleries
- Research papers, grant applications and project reports
- Non-fiction books and essays

## How it works

Send me the text and tell me where it will appear. Within two working days you get a fixed price and a delivery
date. Every translation is read a second time before it goes to you.
