We replaced the single heap with **three wooden bins**. Fresh waste goes in the first bin, the second one rests, and the third one is ready to use.

## The one rule

Chop things small and keep a layer of dry leaves on top. That is all.

## Using the compost

Members may take a bucket for their own plot. Please leave the bins tidy and close the lids.
