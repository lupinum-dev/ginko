Terms of service for software that business customers use online usually cover:

- who the contract is with, and that it is for businesses only
- how the contract comes about: the free trial, choosing a plan, and when the contract starts
- what the service includes, how available it is, and planned maintenance
- prices (net, plus VAT), billing periods, payment and what happens when a payment is late
- changing plans, the term and how to cancel
- what happens to the customer’s data at the end of the contract, and the data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR
- liability and its limits, the place of jurisdiction and the applicable law

The prices on the pricing page and the answers about billing, cancellation and data export must say the same as these terms.
