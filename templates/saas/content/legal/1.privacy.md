This page covers the website. The data your customers keep in your product is a separate matter: describe it in your data processing agreement, not here.

A privacy policy for the website usually covers:

- who is responsible for the website, with address and contact details, and your data protection officer if you must have one
- which personal data the website processes and why, for example server logs at your host and enquiries by email
- the legal basis, and how long you keep the data
- which services you use to run the site (hosting, email) and where they process data
- the rights of visitors: access, correction, deletion, objection, and a complaint to the data protection authority of your federal state

As shipped, this template uses no analytics, sets no tracking cookies, loads nothing from other servers and has no forms: “Start free trial” leads to your app, and enquiries are emails. If you add a service that collects data, describe it here.
