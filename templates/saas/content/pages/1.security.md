## Where your data is stored

Planwerk runs on servers in data centres in Germany, operated by a hosting company based in the EU. We do not transfer personal data to countries outside the EU.

## Who can see it

Each home sees only its own data. Within a home, roles decide what a person sees: staff see their own shifts and wishes, ward leads their ward, and the home's administrators everything. Our support team sees your data only when you ask us for help, and every access is logged.

## How it is protected

Every connection to Planwerk is encrypted. The database is backed up every night; the backups are encrypted and kept in a second data centre in Germany. You can switch on sign-in with a second step (a code from an app) for everyone in your home.

## Data processing agreement

Planwerk processes personal data of your staff on your behalf. Every contract therefore comes with a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR, which lists the measures above and the companies we work with. Write to us if you want to read it before you sign up.

## When you leave

After you cancel, you can export your plans, wishes and hours for 30 days. Then we delete your data, and it leaves the backups within the following 30 days.
