## Help with Planwerk

If you are a customer, look [in the app](https://app.example.com/login) under Help first. If you find no answer there, write to us with the name of your home and what you tried; we answer on working days, usually the same day.

## A quote for several homes

Tell us how many homes or clinics you plan for and roughly how many staff work in each. We send you a quote for the Group plan and suggest a date for a first call.

You find answers about billing, contracts and your data in the [FAQ]($page.faq).
