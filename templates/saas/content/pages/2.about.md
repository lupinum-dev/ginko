## Why Planwerk

Some of us planned shifts in care homes ourselves, on paper, in spreadsheets and on the phone late at night. We started Planwerk to make that part of the job shorter, so ward leads have more time for their teams and residents.

## How we work

We build Planwerk with the homes that use it. Every new feature starts with a visit to a ward, and every release is listed in our [changelog]($page.changelog). We answer support requests ourselves, from our office in Musterstadt.

## What we do not do

We do not sell data, we do not show advertising, and we do not use your plans to train software for others. Planwerk is paid for by its customers, and that is all.
