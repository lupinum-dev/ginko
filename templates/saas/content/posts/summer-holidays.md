Summer is the hardest time to plan: everyone wants the same weeks off, and the plan still needs enough qualified staff on every shift. These five habits help.

1. **Collect wishes early.** Open the holiday wishes in spring and set a date by which everyone enters theirs.
2. **Plan the gaps first.** Put the weeks with the fewest people in the plan before the easy ones.
3. **Share the popular weeks fairly.** Look at who had the school holidays last year before you decide this year.
4. **Plan a reserve.** Keep one person per week who can take a shift at short notice, and say so in the plan.
5. **Publish early.** A plan that is out six weeks ahead leaves time for swaps, before they become emergencies.

In Planwerk, holiday wishes and swaps go through the app, so the plan stays the one place where everything is written down.
