A rota that breaks a rest period is easy to write and hard to fix a week later. That is why Planwerk checks the rules while you plan: each time you put a person on a shift, not when the plan is done.

## What it checks

- the rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next, as set for your home
- the most hours a person may work in a day and in a week, from their contract
- how many qualified staff each shift needs, as set for each ward

A shift that breaks a rule gets a warning with the rule and the person’s name. You can still save the plan: sometimes a ward lead must decide against a rule in an emergency, and the warning stays in the plan’s history.

## What it leaves to you

Planwerk knows the rules you set, not every agreement in your home. Check the settings once with your works council or staff representatives, and ask us if a rule you need is missing.
