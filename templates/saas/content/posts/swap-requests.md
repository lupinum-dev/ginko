In most homes, a swap starts with a phone call to the ward lead, then a few calls to colleagues, and ends with a note on the paper plan that nobody else sees. From version 4.6, Planwerk does this in the app.

## How it works

A person who cannot work a shift opens it in the app and chooses “Ask for a swap”. Planwerk asks only colleagues who have the qualification the shift needs and who keep their rest periods with the new shift. The first one who says yes is put forward to the ward lead, who sees both plans side by side and approves or declines.

## What stays the same

The ward lead still decides. A swap that breaks a rule is never offered, and every swap shows in the plan's history with who asked and who approved.

Swap requests are part of the Standard and Group plans. The [changelog]($page.changelog) lists everything else that changed in 4.6.
