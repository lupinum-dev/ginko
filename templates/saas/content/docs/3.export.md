---
title: Export for payroll
description: Send hours to your payroll provider.
section: Guides
---

At the end of each month, Planwerk adds up the hours of every person, split into day, night and holiday hours.

Choose **Reports**, then **Hours**, then the month. The file is a CSV with one line per person.

Your payroll provider reads this file. If it needs another format, write to support and we add it. The files stay in Germany.
