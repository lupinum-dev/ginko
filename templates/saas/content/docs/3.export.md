---
title: Export for payroll
description: Send the hours of the month to your payroll provider.
section: Guides
---

At the end of each month Planwerk adds up the hours of every person, split into day, night, Sunday and holiday hours.

::steps
### Close the month
Open **Reports**, then **Hours**, and choose the month. Check the totals and close the month; a later change then shows as a correction.

### Download the file
Choose **Export**. The file is a CSV file with one line per person and one column per type of hours.

### Send it to payroll
Upload the file to your payroll software, or send it to your payroll provider.
::

On the Standard plan the export uses the format of your payroll software. If yours is not in the list, write to us and we add it. The files stay on servers in Germany.
