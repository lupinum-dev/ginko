---
title: Shifts and swaps
description: How the rule checks work and how your team swaps shifts.
section: Guides
---

A shift belongs to one ward and one person. Planwerk checks every change against the rules of the ward: the rest time between two shifts, the weekly hours and the minimum staffing.

## Rule checks

A red check names the rule and the person, for example “Anna Weber: 9 hours of rest instead of 11”. Change the shift, or save it with a reason; the reason stays in the rota's history.

::callout{type="warning" title="Changes to a published rota"}
A change to a published rota sends a message to everyone it touches. Look at the checks before you save.
::

## Swaps

Your team asks for a swap in the app. You see the request under **Swaps**, with the checks for both people, and approve or decline it. Planwerk updates the rota and tells both people.

## Wishes

Before you plan a month, your team enters days off and preferred shifts in the app. Planwerk shows the wishes in the rota, so you see which ones your plan meets.
