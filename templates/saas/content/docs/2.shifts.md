A shift belongs to one ward and one person. Planwerk checks every change against the rules of the ward: rest time, weekly hours and the minimum staffing.

::callout{type="warning" title="Published rotas"}
A change to a published rota sends a message to the people it touches. Check the rule results before you save.
::

## Swaps

Staff can ask for a swap in the app. The planner approves it, and Planwerk runs the checks again.

```ts
const swap = await planwerk.swaps.create({ from: 'shift_123', to: 'shift_456' })
console.log(swap.status) // 'pending'
```
