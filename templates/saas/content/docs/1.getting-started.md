Planwerk plans the shifts of your care home or clinic. This page takes you from a new account to the first rota your team sees on their phones. You need the role **Planner**.

::steps
### Add your wards
Open **Teams** and add each ward you plan for. A ward has its own staff list and its own rules, for example how many nurses a night shift needs.

### Invite your team
Choose **Staff**, then **Invite**, and enter the email addresses. Each person gets a link to the app and enters their wishes for the month.

### Plan the month
Open **Rota**, choose the month and drag shifts onto the days. Planwerk checks each shift against the ward's rules as you go.

### Publish
Publish the rota when every check is green. Your team gets a message on their phone.
::

## Shift types

Every new ward starts with these shift types. Change their times under **Teams**, then **Shift types**.

| Shift | Time | Counts as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early | 06:00–14:00 | Day |
| Late | 14:00–22:00 | Day |
| Night | 22:00–06:00 | Night |
| On call | 00:00–24:00 | Standby |
