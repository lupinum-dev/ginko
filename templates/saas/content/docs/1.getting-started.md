Planwerk plans shifts for care homes and small clinics. This page takes you from a new account to a first published rota. You need an account and the role Planner.

Open **Teams** and add the wards you plan for. Each ward has its own staff list and its own rules, for example the minimum number of nurses on a night shift.

## Shift types

| Type | Start | End | Counts as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early | 06:00 | 14:00 | Day |
| Late | 14:00 | 22:00 | Day |
| Night | 22:00 | 06:00 | Night |
| On call | 00:00 | 24:00 | Standby |

Publish the rota when the rule checks are green. Staff get a message on their phone.
