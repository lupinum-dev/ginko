Wishes can now be entered for a whole week at once, and the plan shows the public holidays of your federal state.

### New

- Enter a wish for several days in one step, for example “no early shifts” for a whole week.
- The plan marks the public holidays of the federal state set for your home.

### Improved

- The print view fits a whole month on one A4 page in landscape.

### Fixed

- “Start from last month” also copied the shifts of people who had left.
