Staff can now ask to swap a shift in the app, and the ward lead approves it with one tap.

<!-- more -->

### New

- Swap requests: open a shift in the app and choose “Ask for a swap”. Planwerk asks only colleagues who have the qualification the shift needs and who keep their rest periods.
- The ward lead sees both plans side by side and approves or declines the swap.

### Improved

- The month view of a ward with more than 60 staff opens faster.

### Fixed

- A night shift that ended on the first day of a month was counted in the wrong month of the hours export.
