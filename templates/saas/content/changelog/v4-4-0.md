The hours export now comes in the formats of common payroll programs, and a contract can change in the middle of a month.

<!-- more -->

### New

- Hours export: choose the format of your payroll program, or plain CSV.
- A contract can change on any day: Planwerk counts the hours before and after the change separately.

### Improved

- A rule warning now names the rule and the person, for example “Anna Beispiel: less than 11 hours of rest after the late shift on Tuesday”.
