Planwerk now checks that every shift has enough qualified staff, set per ward.

<!-- more -->

### New

- Set for each ward how many qualified nurses each shift needs. A shift with too few shows a warning in the plan.

### Improved

- Signing in on a phone remembers you for 30 days, unless you sign out.

### Fixed

- Names with umlauts were sorted at the end of the staff list.
