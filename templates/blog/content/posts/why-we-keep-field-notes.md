We have walked together for years: Ida with binoculars, Tom with a tree guide that falls apart a little more each spring. We both write things down. This blog is where those notes go next.

## What you will find here

Short posts, a new one when we have seen something worth telling, on the nature that is close to home: the birds in a back yard, the trees on a street, a walk from a tram stop, a day at the coast. Nothing here needs a car or special gear. Most of it you can see on your way to work.

We write what we saw and how we found it. When we are not sure what something was, we say so.

## Why notes, not photos

A photo keeps what was in front of the lens. A note keeps what you paid attention to: the time, the weather, the sound you could not place. Read back after a year, notes show the seasons turning in a way a camera roll does not.

So most posts here have no photo. They have a place, a date and what happened.
