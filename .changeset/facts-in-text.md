Add `facts` in ginko.config: texts quote fields of single bricks with `:fact{name="practice.price"}`, and pages, site search, Markdown twins and llms-full.txt show the value in effect today (a planned change from its date). The Studio shows such a component as a chip with the value the page shows, and any inline component without text of its own as a chip instead of an empty box. Also `useGinkoFact()` and `valueOn()`.
